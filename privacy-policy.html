Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 18, 2026

Anatomy Atlas is an educational 3D anatomy reference app. This policy explains what information may be processed when you use the app.

Data Collection

Anatomy Atlas does not require account registration and does not operate its own server-side user profile system.

The app may store local settings on your device, such as interface preferences, selected display options, and learning mode state. This information stays on your device and is not uploaded by Anatomy Atlas.

Advertising

Anatomy Atlas uses Google Mobile Ads to display ads. Ad requests are configured as non-personalized in the app code. Google Mobile Ads may process information such as device identifiers, approximate network-derived location, ad interaction data, crash data, performance data, and diagnostic data to deliver, measure, and protect ads.

Google's handling of advertising data is governed by Google's own policies and technologies.

Medical Disclaimer

Anatomy Atlas is for education and reference only. It does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or professional medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical concerns.

Model Sources and Licenses

3D anatomy model sources and license attributions are provided inside the app and in project attribution files. Anatomy Atlas is intended to ship only assets that are permitted for public redistribution.

Children

Anatomy Atlas is an educational reference app and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features, advertising configuration, or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions or correction requests, use the support contact listed on the App Store page for Anatomy Atlas.
